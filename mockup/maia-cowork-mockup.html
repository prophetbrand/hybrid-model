Change log

Numbers match the callouts on the mockup.

  1. Brand mark. The Lyzr mark and "OpenCowork · Beta" are replaced by the Prophet P mark, the MAIA wordmark and a small V2 tag.
  2. Bottom-left link block (new). External MAIA, Agent Studio, Architect and Help & Info sit above the org switcher and user, styled like the MAIA left rail.
  3. Design system chip. The composer defaults to the "Prophet" design system instead of Meridian, backed by the design system MCP.
  4. Suggested rows. Suggestions point at Prophet skills, endorsed agents, document chat and client spaces rather than generic Cowork starters.
  5. Primary colour. Cowork's brown primary is replaced by MAIA blue rgb(20,124,255) on the send button and all CTAs; type moves to Graphik (Inter shown here).
